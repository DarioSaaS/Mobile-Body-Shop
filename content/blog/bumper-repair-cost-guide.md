If you've recently backed into a pole, been tapped in a parking lot, or noticed a spiderweb of cracks spreading across your front bumper, your first question is probably: how much is this going to cost me? The honest answer is that bumper repair pricing varies widely depending on the severity of the damage, the material of your bumper, and whether paint-matching is required.

## Typical Bumper Repair Price Ranges

Pricing generally falls into three tiers based on damage severity:

- **Minor scuffs and small cracks**: $150–$400 for mobile repair, covering sanding, filling, priming, and paint blending so the repair is invisible.
- **Larger cracks requiring plastic welding or reinforcement mesh**: $400–$700.
- **Full bumper cover replacement** (swapping the entire plastic shell): $800–$1,500 once labor, parts, and paint are factored in.

## Why Most Bumpers Can Be Repaired, Not Replaced

The good news is that a large percentage of bumper damage doesn't require full replacement. Modern bumper covers are made of flexible thermoplastic, which means many cracks and punctures can be professionally repaired rather than replaced. This is where mobile paintless dent repair (PDR) technicians add real value: we assess the damage on-site, often within the same day, and give you an honest recommendation instead of automatically upselling a replacement part.

## Does Insurance Cover Bumper Repair?

If the damage came from a collision with another vehicle, your claim may cover the full repair cost minus your deductible. However, for minor cosmetic damage — a shopping cart scrape, a low-speed backing incident — many customers find it's cheaper and faster to pay out of pocket for a mobile repair than to file a claim and risk a premium increase.

## What Happens During a Mobile Bumper Repair Appointment

Our technician arrives at your home or office with a fully stocked van and follows a consistent process:

1. Assess the crack or scuff and confirm it's a good candidate for repair.
2. Sand and prep the damaged surface.
3. Apply a flexible filler designed specifically for plastic bumpers.
4. Color-match and blend the paint using your vehicle's exact factory code.

Most repairs are completed in two to four hours and are dry to the touch before we leave.
